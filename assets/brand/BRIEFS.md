## 2. A dedicated small icon (designer)

Every size, including the 16–32 px slots in the ICO and favicon files, is currently scaled from
`catcup-icon.svg`. A separate 16–32 px drawing should keep the tile, the coffee disc, the cat's head
outline and the cup rim, and drop the bubbles, whiskers and cheeks so it stays legible at 16 px.
Deliver it as an SVG and it will be embedded into the small ICO and favicon slots.
